# Custom Keycaps

Custom keycaps replace a keyboard's existing caps with a chosen legend.

## Language

**Keycap**:
The removable cap mounted on a keyboard switch.
_Avoid_: Key, switch

**Lighting keycap**:
The 1u keycap at the top-right of the user's Keychron K2, used for the keyboard's lighting control.

**Body**:
The structural part of a keycap, including its shell and switch attachment.

**Stem**:
The keycap's internal boss surrounding the stem socket and joining it to the roof; distinct from the switch's moving stem.

**Stem socket**:
The opening on the underside of the keycap that receives the keyboard switch's stem.
_Avoid_: Switch

**Front**:
The side of the keycap facing the typist, toward the spacebar, when installed.

**Rear**:
The side of the keycap facing away from the typist, toward the monitor, when installed.
_Avoid_: Back, when referring to this side

**Legend**:
The visible icon on a keycap, formed from a separate material region inlaid flush with the body surface.
_Avoid_: Image, decoration

**1u**:
The standard single-key width unit in a keyboard layout; it does not specify an exact physical measurement.
